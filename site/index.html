Regional Distribution

A vast majority of tracked time and data points are concentrated in the Southwest region (Oyo, Ogun, Lagos, Ekiti), reflecting long-term living and work bases. Visits to the South-South (Rivers, Edo) and the North (Kaduna, Abuja, Kogi) represent distinct chapters of travel for education, family, and specific career events.
